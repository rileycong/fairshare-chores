Help

Getting started

Create a household on the welcome screen with your name, a 4–8 digit PIN, and your WhatsApp number. You'll get a join code to share.

Roommates join by entering that code with their own name, PIN, and WhatsApp number. A household holds 2–4 roommates. Your device remembers you — no passwords anywhere.

How assignment works

Assignment is automatic — there is no manual reassignment. Every new chore occurrence goes to the roommate with the fewest completed effort points. If two roommates are tied, the tie rotates so everyone gets a fair turn.

Effort points are fixed per chore size: small = 1, medium = 2, large = 3. The next occurrence is always scheduled from the original due date — completing late never shifts the rota.

Marking a chore done

On the Chores screen, your own chores show a Mark done button. Completing a chore adds its points to your total, writes a permanent history entry, restocks any linked supply, and schedules the next occurrence. Only the current assignee can complete a chore.

Chores past their due date show as Overdue and stay visible until the assignee completes them.

Swapping a chore

On your chore card, pick a roommate in the dropdown and press Request swap. The chore shows as "Swap requested" until they respond.

The chosen roommate sees the request in Swap requests for you at the top of their Chores screen and can Accept or Decline. Accepting moves the chore to them — nobody else can approve it, and there is no other way to reassign.

Supplies

The Supplies screen lists household items and their restock status. Anyone can toggle an item between restocked and not-restocked. Link a supply to a chore in the chore editor — completing that chore (e.g. "Buy toilet paper") automatically marks the supply restocked.

History

The History screen shows every completed chore in the household, newest first, with who did it, the points earned, and whether the assignment was swapped.

Reminders

Each chore has its own reminder time. When it arrives, the assignee gets a WhatsApp message and a web push notification.

Enable web push on the Settings screen. On WhatsApp, reply done to the bot to complete your earliest due chore — the bot only accepts replies from the assigned roommate's number.

Settings is also where you change your display name, PIN, or WhatsApp number, and where you can find the household join code again.

AI assistant

The Ask the assistant box on the Chores screen can explain why an assignment is fair, suggest who is next, draft a new chore ("add vacuuming every two weeks"), or draft a swap ("swap my chore with Ben").

Chore and swap drafting works even without an AI API key — the assistant parses simple requests like "add clean the bathroom large every month at 7pm". The confirmation page shows who will be assigned: always the roommate with the fewest completed points, chosen automatically.

Anything that changes data always shows a confirmation page first — nothing is saved until you confirm, and cancelling saves nothing. Swap drafts still need the target roommate's approval.
